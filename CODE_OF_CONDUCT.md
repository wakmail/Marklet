# Code of Conduct

Marklet is a small project. This is the short version of how we expect people to behave here, in issues, pull
requests, discussions, and anywhere else the project is being worked on.

## What we expect

* Be decent to people. Assume they are trying to help.
* Keep criticism about the work, not the person.
* Accept that maintainers may say no to an idea, and that a no is not an insult.
* Respect that people here have different levels of experience, different first languages, and different amounts
  of free time.

## What is not welcome

* Harassment, insults, or personal attacks.
* Demeaning comments about who someone is, including their background, appearance, disability, gender, race,
  religion, or sexuality.
* Sexual attention or imagery.
* Publishing private information about someone without their clear permission.
* Persistent disruption after being asked to stop.

## Scope

This applies in every project space, and it also applies when someone is publicly representing the project, for
example posting from a project account.

## Reporting

Report anything that concerns you to hello@getmarklet.com. Reports go to the maintainer and stay private. Tell us
what happened, where, and roughly when, and include links if you have them.

You will get a reply within a week. We will not discuss a report with the person it concerns before speaking with
you, unless you ask us to.

## What happens after a report

Depending on what happened, the response may be a private word, a public correction, edited or removed content, a
temporary block, or a permanent ban from the project. We aim to be proportionate and we will tell you what we
decided.

If you disagree with a decision that affects you, reply to the same address and say why. One person maintains this
project, so an appeal is a second look rather than a separate panel, and we would rather hear you out than have you
leave quietly.
